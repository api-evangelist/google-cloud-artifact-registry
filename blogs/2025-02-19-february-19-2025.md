v1 Issue Artifact Registry might give a 400 error on pushes or pulls for Workforce Identity Federation users. This issue is caused by Workforce Identity Federation attribute mappings in the Artifact Registry URL causing problems on the backend. To mitigate this issue, you can push or pull from Artifact Registry without attribute mappings, or reduce the length of your attribute mappings.
